# gold-script-lua
its a gold script for lua 

all functions

gold_check() - check your total gold    
gold_add(gold_input) - add gold to your total gold    
gold_delete(gold_input) - delete gold from your total gold    
gold_reset() - reset your total gold to 0    
confirm() - confirm a gold reset or going into negative gold    

tipp

you need to put a number in the gold_input slots like gold_add(100) or gold_delete(200)
